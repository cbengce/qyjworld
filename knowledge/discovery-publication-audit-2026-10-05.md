8. Readability: concise purchasing tasks, everyday language, clearly named headings, Chinese author/category/reading labels. No minimum word target.
9. Grammar/style: British English kept; Chinese is written for readers rather than transliterating English headings. Formal brand lines retained as approved English phrases.
10. Cultural accuracy: Modern Oriental tea is identified as brand positioning. The category framework is attributed and not claimed as QYJ's complete menu or all historical tea culture.
11. Practical usefulness: clear menu, visit and group-order paths; Chinese search; same-page language switching; actual availability/prices used; no imaginary packages or fulfilment guarantees.
12. Brand voice: warm, clear, modern; official logo untouched. Owner photographs diversify the existing image set.

Core public navigation/home sections, categories/search, contact and editorial labels are localised.
