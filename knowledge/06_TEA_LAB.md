# Tea Lab

Tea Lab is the first-hand evidence system for product and tasting observations. Records must describe actual tests performed by identified people. AI-generated sensory experience is prohibited.

AI must never write “we tested”, “we found”, or “our tasting showed” unless a corresponding Tea Lab record exists.

Do not infer missing fields. A record without product version, tester, and date cannot support a first-hand public claim.
