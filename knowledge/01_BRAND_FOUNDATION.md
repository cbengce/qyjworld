- Address: 401 MacPherson Road, MacPherson Mall, Singapore 368125

## Approved Brand Interpretation

The name QING YUN JIAN and the Pegasus symbol express upward movement, aspiration, and the idea of rising towards the clouds. This supports a calm, hopeful, upward-looking expression of a Modern Oriental tea experience.

The brand purpose may be described carefully as creating a contemporary Singapore tea experience informed by Oriental tea culture. This is an approved positioning statement, not a claim of historical authority, product superiority, or medical benefit.
