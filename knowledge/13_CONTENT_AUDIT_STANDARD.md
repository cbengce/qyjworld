# Content Audit Standard

Passing lint and build does not mean content is editorially publishable.

## Audit dimensions

Score and document evidence for each dimension:

1. Factual accuracy
2. Invented brand facts or history
3. Source quality and claim-to-source alignment
4. First-hand experience and evidence
5. Health, legal, nutritional, investment, and regulatory risk
6. Exact and near-duplicate content
7. Keyword stuffing and unnatural search phrasing
8. Readability for the intended audience
9. Grammar and British English consistency
10. Cultural and historical accuracy
11. Practical usefulness and unique value
12. QING YUN JIAN brand voice
13. Publication readiness

Readability scores are diagnostic, not automatic pass/fail rules. Review sentence length, vocabulary, information density, navigation, and audience familiarity together.

## Decisions

| Decision | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PUBLISH | Accurate, useful, approved, and ready for technical validation |
| MINOR EDITS | Fundamentally sound; limited corrections do not change the core argument |
| SUBSTANTIAL REVISION | Useful foundation, but important sourcing, structure, accuracy, or originality work remains |
| FULL REWRITE | The current structure or prose cannot be responsibly repaired through local edits |
| REMOVE OR ARCHIVE | Misleading, obsolete, duplicative without unique value, or strategically inappropriate |

## Mandatory audit record

Record content ID, version, auditor, date, findings by dimension, required reviewers, decision, remediation owner, and approval evidence. Never invent a score or approval to complete the form.
