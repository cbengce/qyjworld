# Brand Values

The labels below are governance labels. Proposed values must not be presented publicly as final until founder confirmation is recorded.

## APPROVED

### Born to Ascend

Use the approved tagline as an upward-looking invitation to learn, improve, and move with hope. It is not a performance guarantee.

## PROPOSED — FOUNDER CONFIRMATION REQUIRED

### Tea First

Let tea remain identifiable in product decisions and communication.

### Culture Before Trend

Approach Oriental tea culture with respect, context, and care rather than using it as decoration.

### Modern Oriental Expression

Interpret tea for contemporary Singapore while distinguishing modern creativity from historical tradition.

### Thoughtful Craft

Make deliberate choices and explain only those supported by product or Tea Lab records.

### Community

Create warm, respectful ways for guests and supporters to participate without overstating belonging or benefits.

### Honesty

State what is known, label what is uncertain, and avoid exaggerated claims.

### Continuous Improvement

Use documented feedback and testing to improve, without inventing results or claiming perfection.

Founder approval, date, owner, and permitted public wording are required before changing any proposed value to approved.
