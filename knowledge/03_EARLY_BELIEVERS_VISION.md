# Early Believers Vision

“Early Believers” describes people who feel aligned with the long-term craft, culture, community, and responsible use of technology around QING YUN JIAN. It is not a public investment solicitation and must not be presented as one.

## Approved framing

- Aligned supporters can help a young brand listen, learn, and improve over time.
- Long-term brand building requires patience, consistency, honest feedback, and cultural care.
- Tea, community, culture, and technology may be discussed as areas of interest, not guaranteed commercial outcomes.
- Shared belief means alignment with purpose and standards; it must not imply entitlement to financial participation or returns.
