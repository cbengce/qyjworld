# Read This First

This knowledge base is the highest editorial authority in this repository. It governs website articles, guides, product descriptions, brand pages, promotion copy, social copy, structured brand data, founder material, and AI-generated content.

## Mandatory contributor rules

Every AI or human contributor must:

1. Read the relevant knowledge files before drafting or editing.
2. Use only verified facts for factual claims.
3. Distinguish verified facts, approved interpretation, and requested founder or product input.
4. Mark uncertainty visibly; never conceal it with confident wording.
5. Never invent brand history, founder biography, results, awards, sourcing claims, scientific claims, customer numbers, store counts, investment amounts, or expansion plans.
6. Avoid unsupported health, nutritional, medical, investment, ranking, and competitor claims.
7. Preserve the approved QING YUN JIAN identity and British English editorial style.
8. Use Tea Lab evidence only when a real Tea Lab record exists.
9. Run the editorial audit in `13_CONTENT_AUDIT_STANDARD.md` before publication.
10. Obtain the required human, founder, expert, or legal approval for the relevant publication state.

## Conflict rule

When repository content conflicts with the verified Fact Register, stop and report the conflict instead of silently choosing one version. Do not overwrite either version until an authorised owner resolves the conflict and the register is updated.

Passing lint, tests, or a production build is not evidence that content is factually accurate or editorially publishable.
