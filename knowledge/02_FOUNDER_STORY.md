## Founder interview questionnaire

1. What parts of your life and professional experience are relevant to QING YUN JIAN?
2. What first made tea personally meaningful to you?
3. What specific problem or opportunity prompted you to create the brand?
4. Why did you choose the name QING YUN JIAN and what does each part mean to you?
5. Why did the brand begin in 2026?
6. Why was Singapore the right place to begin?
7. What does “Modern Oriental tea” mean in your own words?
8. How was the Pegasus selected, and what should it communicate?
9. Why was MacPherson Mall selected for the first store?
10. Which decisions before opening were most difficult, and why?
11. Which early assumptions changed after opening?
12. What have guests taught you so far? Provide dates or records where possible.
13. Which parts of tea culture do you most want to preserve?
14. Which parts do you want to express differently for contemporary guests?
15. What should the brand never compromise?
16. What do you hope QING YUN JIAN will contribute to Singapore?
17. Which ambitions are approved for public communication now?
18. Which details must remain private or require legal review?

Interview answers must be dated, approved by the founder, and entered into the Source and Fact Registers before publication.
