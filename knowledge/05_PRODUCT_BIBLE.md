# Product Bible

## Authority and use

This register reflects live production product data where it is clear. Backup files are not stronger evidence than current production data. Descriptions below are product-copy records, not independently verified recipes. Recipe, allergen, sourcing, nutritional, and benefit claims require product-team evidence.

Permitted general wording: product name, listed category, displayed price, and the current production description when unchanged and current.

Prohibited for every product unless separately substantiated: medical benefit, disease prevention or treatment, “healthiest”, “best”, “detox”, “boosts immunity”, guaranteed result, undisclosed allergen claim, or superiority over all bubble tea.
