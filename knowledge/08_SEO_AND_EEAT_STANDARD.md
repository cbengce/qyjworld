# SEO and EEAT Standard

Search visibility must follow useful, accurate content. Length is determined by user intent and completeness, not a fixed minimum.

Every content type requires unique value, original viewpoint, accurate sourcing, readable structure, no programmatic section repetition, no padding, appropriate author identity, and an updated date only after substantive revision.

## Content-type requirements

| Type | Search intent and depth | Sources and first-hand evidence | Links and technical requirements | Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand journal | Brand discovery; focused narrative | Fact Register plus approved founder or operational source; first-hand evidence for claimed events | Relevant brand/product links; unique metadata, canonical, Article schema; original image where useful | Editorial and founder review when personal or strategic |
| Product story | Product discovery and meaning | Product Bible; Tea Lab for tasting language; approved image and current availability | Product/menu links; unique metadata and canonical; Product or Article schema as appropriate | Product-team and editorial review |
| Ingredient explainer | Informational understanding | Reliable primary or authoritative references; product records for brand-specific use | Relevant product and education links; descriptive image; FAQ only for genuine recurring questions | Editorial; expert review for health/safety |
| Local guide | Local discovery and visit planning | Current official location sources and first-hand verification; dated checks | Contact, map, menu and related guide links; LocalBusiness relevance; useful original images | Local fact check and editorial review |
| Tea education article | Educational intent | Authoritative tea references; distinguish consensus, regional practice, and opinion | Related guides/products where natural; Article and breadcrumb schema | Subject review when specialised |
| Health-adjacent article | Safe, evidence-led explanation | Current authoritative or primary evidence; no unsupported inference; first-hand tasting cannot establish health effect | Clear scope and appropriate external references; FAQ only if evidence supports answers | Qualified expert and claims review required |
| Pillar guide | Comprehensive navigation of a broad topic | Multiple appropriate sources and original synthesis; first-hand evidence where experience is claimed | Strong table of contents and natural internal links; unique metadata, canonical, Article and breadcrumb schema | Senior editorial and relevant expert review |

## Common technical checklist

- Match one clear search intent without repeating the target phrase unnaturally.
- Use a unique title and description that accurately represent the page.
- Set one canonical URL.
- Use Open Graph and social metadata based on the actual content.
- Use schema only when visible content satisfies the schema.
- Give images descriptive, non-duplicated alt text; set dimensions or responsive sizing.
- Link internally only where it helps the reader.
- Do not manufacture FAQs or sources to satisfy a checklist.
- Display an accountable author identity where appropriate.
