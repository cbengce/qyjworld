# Repository Agent Instructions

## Editorial and Brand Content

Before modifying blog articles, guides, About or Story pages, product descriptions, promotion copy, social links, structured brand data, or founder or investor content, Codex and all other agents must read:

- `knowledge/00_READ_FIRST.md`
- `knowledge/11_FACT_REGISTER.md`
- `knowledge/07_EDITORIAL_VOICE.md`
- `knowledge/09_LEGAL_AND_CLAIMS_STANDARD.md`

Also read any topic-specific knowledge file and current product or source record relevant to the change.

Do not invent facts or silently resolve conflicts. When repository content conflicts with the Fact Register, stop and report the conflict. Preserve British English for official English-language editorial content and complete the editorial audit before publication.
