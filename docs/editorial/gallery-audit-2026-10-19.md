## Findings against the 13 editorial dimensions

1. Factual accuracy: new captions describe visible artwork and verified files; no new offer eligibility or operating hours.
2. Invented history: no founder quotations, performance, dates or results invented.
3. Source alignment: originals visually inspected; pixels and DPI read from the files. Filenames alone do not prove print resolution.
4. First-hand evidence: actual shop photographs distinguished from illustrations.
5. Regulated claims: original fitness posters contain nutrition and health wording not independently substantiated here. Archive pages explicitly say these are earlier artwork, not verified nutrition data or current offers. No such claims are repeated in new captions, metadata or structured data. Do not reuse these posters for a new live health/nutrition campaign without the review required by the claims standard.
6. Duplication: one public final per design; earlier drafts are retained in the owner's recovered-original bundle rather than crowding the gallery. Separate language routes have corresponding canonicals/hreflang.
7. Search phrasing: descriptive titles, no rankings or keyword stuffing.
8. Readability: English title above Chinese title; captions and UI localised.
9. Language: British English, restrained captions, proper names preserved.
10. Cultural accuracy: Pegasus is the approved brand symbol; no invented folklore or religious claims. The pictured Pokémon card pack is part of the original TEFUDA campaign artwork, not a claim of endorsement.
11. Usefulness: media/topic/product filters, full compositions, captions and links to menu and group ordering.
12. Brand voice: bright Pegasus work, unchanged official logos, concrete captions.
13. Publication readiness: seeded poster/photo works ready. Video publishing is supported in CMS, but there are no recovered video files in this initial public collection; do not invent a video or imply all past material has been found.

Required reviewers: owner for this historical-artwork collection, already authorised. Any new live offer or regulated claim still requires its applicable factual and qualified review. No expert approval has been invented.
