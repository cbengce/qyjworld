# Chapter Six: More Than a Cup to Hold
## The four founding purposes of QINGYUNJIAN

If someone asks us one day why we founded QINGYUNJIAN, we hope our answer begins with people.

People who have retired and still want to participate, with experience they still have to offer. People in the middle of their lives, carrying responsibilities and a dream they have not yet finished. Young people standing at the doorway to their first business, full of enthusiasm but unsure where to begin. And people who want to take more thoughtful care of themselves in everyday life.

In our vision, these people were never an afterthought. Our four founding purposes begin with the question of whom this business should serve. If we can build a working commercial system, who should gain more opportunities through it? Could those opportunities reach beyond the few who are already ahead?

Tea is where we have chosen to begin. From here, we hope to build a business that can gradually carry these wishes.

### A life worth participating in, after retirement

Retirement may mean leaving a role. It should not mean losing the opportunity to participate in the world.

Experience remains. Judgement remains. An understanding of people remains. So do the patience, responsibility and sense of proportion gathered over many years. Our hope for older people is to consider seriously how they might participate in the long-term value of a real operating business.

Our original proposal called this direction a retirement passive-income support plan. Behind that working title was a wish: that the later part of a person's life could remain connected to a growing endeavour. We hope to explore forms of participation that reduce the burden of day-to-day operations while respecting each person's choices, abilities and capacity for risk.

That wish must rest on real operations. A shop needs to be run well, costs understood, cash flow sustained and arrangements clearly defined. Any form of participation still requires further design and review. Good intentions cannot turn returns that have not been achieved into a guarantee.

Precisely because promises cannot be made lightly, we must take the work seriously. A business that wants to offer opportunities should strive to deserve people's trust. Years from now, we hope QINGYUNJIAN can respond to more than what someone used to do. We hope it can also ask what they would still like to do together.

### Another chapter in the middle of life

Professionals in midlife often carry a wealth of ability alongside a weight of responsibility.

They understand work and their industries. They know how to bring order to a difficult task. They have already given many years, yet may not have the spare capacity to start again from nothing and shoulder every uncertainty of a second business alone.

Our second founding purpose is to explore a path towards a second career and a second source of income, where experience can find renewed use. We want to build a system that can make room for professional ability: a shared brand, learnable processes, management with a clear basis and data people can understand. What someone brings should include their judgement, organisational skills, relationships and responsibility, alongside any financial commitment.

We hope that, one day, a person will not need to discard everything they have been in order to move towards what comes next. The road already travelled should become a source of strength for the next journey.

A new business still requires learning, effort and responsibility. Our task is to turn the experience we can accumulate into clear rules, and recurring problems into processes we can improve, so that those who join later face less unnecessary uncertainty.

What we cherish is the possibility of still being able to say: there are things I want to do, and I am still willing to begin.

### Somewhere for a young person's enthusiasm to take root

When young people first think of starting a business, enthusiasm is precious. It is also easily worn away.

Between an idea and a shop that can keep operating lie products, training, purchasing, service, staffing, rent, food compliance, cash flow and the small problems that arise every day. Any one of them can come at a heavy cost to someone who is unprepared.

That is why our purpose of supporting young entrepreneurship and passing on knowledge centres on gradually building a brand, standard operating procedures and a platform. We hope to turn what earlier participants have learnt into something the next generation can understand, use and continue to improve.

Passing something on involves more than handing over a name or a menu. It should include why a task is done a certain way, how to make decisions when problems appear, where innovation is welcome and which principles must be protected. What deserves to be passed on is the ability to turn enthusiasm into sustained action.

QINGYUNJIAN must undergo its own validation too. We cannot present a system that is still developing as a ready-made answer. We can start with the first shop, record carefully, face problems honestly and gradually organise what we learn.

If young people in the future can avoid some unnecessary detours because of that work, and use the energy they save for products, service and new ideas, this endeavour will mean something beyond ourselves. We hope to leave firmer ground for those who come next.

### Bringing care for ourselves into everyday life

Our fourth founding purpose is broader health and wellbeing.

If that idea remains only a grand concept, it can feel far from daily life. We want to ask whether people's concern for quality of life, food choices and self-care can find a response in experiences they encounter more frequently and can participate in more readily.

Tea offers a starting point. It can connect with different situations in people's lives and leave room for further product exploration. Uplift, in our proposal, was an exploration around the post-exercise setting: an attempt to find new connections between tea and everyday needs.

We value that direction and must also respect the boundaries of what we say. A drink should not be presented as treatment, and a vision cannot replace nutritional evidence. Any future description of a recipe, nutrition or function should rest on real information and the relevant standards.

Our wish is to make caring for ourselves a choice we can consider thoughtfully in everyday life. To give a contemporary expression of tea a place in more people's days. To make every product improvement worthy of the cup we place in a guest's hands.

### A purpose needs a business that can stand

Older people, professionals in midlife, young entrepreneurs, and health and wellbeing: behind these four directions lies a shared question. How do we make our intentions last?

Moving words alone cannot do it. Nor can a founder's enthusiasm, however strong. There must be real products, reasons for consumers to choose them, operations that can be sustained and a system that keeps improving.

Our proposal therefore brings together physical operations, cash flow, standardisation, brand assets and long-term organisational ability. These may sound like the language of commerce, but they matter to whether our purpose can survive the hardest periods.

If we hope to help others, we must first do the work of building this business well. If we hope to create opportunities for more people, we must take every cost, every commitment and every unproven assumption seriously. A purpose needs people to protect it. Operational discipline is one way we do that.

We want QINGYUNJIAN to become an endeavour that can accumulate knowledge, be passed on and make room for more participants. The wish is large; the first step is specific. Make the cup in front of us well. Do the task in front of us well. Keep the people willing to trust us in mind.

Born to Ascend. For us, that direction should include those travelling alongside us.

If QINGYUNJIAN can go further in the years ahead, we hope the road will include people who still want to participate after retirement, people opening another door in midlife, and young people bringing their enthusiasm to the next stage. We hope it will also include ordinary guests finding a reason to return, sit for a while and enjoy a cup of tea.

That is the purpose we hope to protect: as the business moves forward, people have an opportunity to move forward with it.

# Chapter Seven: Let This Cup Travel Further
## From our own brand, to a network of people, to the world

A dream is always a little lighter to speak aloud than to carry into practice.

From a cup of tea, to a membership network, to a global consumer brand: this is the direction set out in QINGYUNJIAN's vision proposal. On paper, it is a clear line. In real life, it must be joined together, step by step, through many ordinary days.

We hope to reach the world. We also know that we must first stand up to the test of the cup in front of us. Why does a guest come? Why would they return? Why would a partner continue working with us? How can a second shop be better prepared than the first? Those questions will determine how far the dream can travel.

### Why begin with tea?

We chose tea because we needed a starting point where we could meet consumers directly, keep learning and gradually establish standards.

Tea can belong in everyday life and carry an exchange between Oriental culture and contemporary tastes. Sparkling Tea Reimagined is our way of exploring that exchange. We hope people come because of the product: that those familiar with tea discover a new expression, and those who seldom drink it find a reason to begin.

Starting with a drink allows us to learn how products, equipment, training, service and a shop work together. We can observe needs in different settings and test whether a process is clear and a standard can genuinely be repeated.

Tea is the first doorway. That doorway must have value in its own right. However many layers we build in the future, the cup a guest holds must never become a supporting character in a grand story. It is our most direct encounter with the consumer, and the beginning of every relationship of trust that follows.

### Why put our own name on it?

Our early exploration included external brands and forms of collaboration. They offered a possible way into the market and gradually brought longer-term questions into focus. How would products evolve? How would the brand develop? How would the supply chain be built and expansion arranged? Who would ultimately own the assets being created?

Choosing to establish our own brand meant accepting greater responsibility. A road already travelled by someone else could not complete QINGYUNJIAN's own validation for us.

We wanted the work we put in today to accumulate within this name. Every product correction, every piece of training, every partnership and every guest willing to return could become part of QINGYUNJIAN's long-term foundation.

We hope consumers eventually remember QINGYUNJIAN itself: both a way of expressing tea and the way we treat them. Owning our brand puts direction and responsibility in the same place. We can decide where to go, and must answer for each step we take.

### The first shop brings the vision into contact with reality

MacPherson Mall is the location of our first shop. It brought the brand from paper into operation and made us face an important reality: having products ready does not mean a steady flow of guests will appear by itself.

Our proposal records limited natural footfall at the first shop and a period of continuing to test channels. It made us rethink growth. Alongside asking how many people pass the door, we needed to ask where the people we want to serve are, how they discover us and whether they have a reason to return after their first purchase.

Schools, fitness settings, hotels, companies, communities, group buying, social media and delivery became directions to explore. The partner network and the shop's ability to fulfil orders needed to be understood together.

Facing reality sometimes means changing an earlier idea. The vision is worth holding onto; the method must accept the test of facts. We hope to have the courage to see problems and the patience to solve them one by one. Every honest correction brings the business a step closer to real life.

### Giving a purchase the chance to become a relationship

A membership network is an important layer of our vision.

We hope a relationship has room to continue after a transaction ends. Guests should return because the products, experience and membership value remain worth choosing. The savings, clarity of benefits and consistency of service all need to be calibrated through real operations.

The membership relationship we want to build should be a continuing invitation: to discuss new products, hear new needs and share a good experience with people close to us. The referral model explores direct, single-tier relationships. Whether members participate must rest on their own choice.

Our proposal envisages a long-term membership scale of fifty thousand, eighty thousand and eventually more than one hundred thousand. These are ambitions that must be demonstrated in stages. Each person who joins remains an individual. We hope respect for people will endure as the scale grows.

A further part of the vision is for relevant partner businesses to offer members meaningful choices, gradually creating value through collaborations beyond tea. Member consent, privacy and relevance are prerequisites. A network worthy of trust should let the people within it know that their choices are respected.

### Let partners see the road we have travelled together

The first cup brought through a partner deserves care too.

Our collaborations with Tefuda, Anytime and IBIS connect different everyday settings with QINGYUNJIAN. We hope these relationships can continue. Continuity needs a clear basis: which purchases came through a partner, how many cups they brought, and how sales, discounts and commissions are recorded.

QR codes, orders, the point-of-sale system and the dashboard help join these questions together. Behind the technology is a simple wish: that each contribution can be seen, and both sides have a common basis for understanding what happened.

A partner network should grow beyond the excitement of a single promotion. We hope real data will help us improve relevant benefits, recognise valuable collaborations and honestly acknowledge attempts that need adjustment. Lasting relationships need warmth and clarity.

### Leave knowledge where those who follow can reach it

Moving from the first shop to a second and third involves more than reproducing the decor and menu.

Products, service, training, supply chains, membership, partnerships, permissions and finance need to become a system people can understand, carry out and check. A founder's experience should gradually become usable rules, rather than remain indefinitely in one person's mind.

This connects directly with our purposes of passing knowledge to young entrepreneurs and creating opportunities for professionals in midlife. The clearer the system, the more room later participants have to use their abilities. They can begin with accumulated knowledge and bring new experience to the endeavour.

We hope a second shop can demonstrate that improved methods are repeatable, and a third can gradually show that operations do not need a founder to resolve every emergency personally. When a team can accept responsibility and an organisation can keep learning, a brand has the foundations to go further.

### From Singapore, towards the world with respect

Our vision proposal envisages building products, service, data and operating standards through company-owned shops in Singapore, then researching overseas franchising, regional partnerships or joint ventures.

It also sets out a direction for exploring markets in stages: first Malaysia, then consideration of Japan and Korea, followed by Europe and the United States, and an assessment of China when the brand and organisation are more mature. That direction remains subject to market research, food regulations, trademarks, supply chains, partner capabilities and real operating conditions.

Reaching the world should involve more than placing our logo in more locations. We hope to bring a contemporary Oriental expression with roots of its own and a willingness to learn, taking the people of each place seriously.

The proposal sets out a long-term capital pathway of seven to eight years, with possibilities including a public listing, strategic acquisition or continued private development. These are choices to assess as evidence, operations and market conditions evolve. We hope scale, profitability, governance and audit readiness can mature gradually, giving this endeavour more paths it can consider seriously.

The world is large. We are willing to imagine it with passion, and learn from it with humility.

### Let the purpose withstand the long days

Our vision contains layers of shops, members, partners, an international brand, supply chains and systems. Each layer should rest on the real value created by the one before it.

If a shop has not yet found firm ground, we need to keep improving. If membership value has not been demonstrated, we need to adjust. If a new market is not ready, we should wait. Capital should support capabilities that have been validated. Operating discipline helps us retain room to choose again when circumstances are difficult.

These requirements do not diminish our passion. They give that passion a chance to last.

We hope QINGYUNJIAN can one day become a brand that begins in Singapore and is thoughtfully chosen in more places. We also hope that, when that day comes, we will still remember our first question: whom should this business ultimately serve?

Opportunities for older people, another chapter for professionals in midlife, knowledge passed to young entrepreneurs, and health and wellbeing brought into everyday life should remain part of future decisions. Shops may increase and the system may grow. Someone should always remember the original wish to make room for people.

From a cup of tea to a system; from a belief to an endeavour many people are willing to carry forward together. We hope to have the courage to speak our dream aloud and the perseverance to place it within our actions every day.

Born to Ascend.

May we remember why we began each time we rise. And when we reach further places, may we still recognise the people we first hoped to bring along.
