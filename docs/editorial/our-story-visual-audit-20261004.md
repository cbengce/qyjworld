# Our Story visual presentation audit

Content ID: `/en/about`, `/zh/about`, story directory.
Version: 4 October 2026 visual refresh.
Auditor and remediation owner: Codex.
Required reviewer: brand owner for brand presentation.
Approval evidence: owner requested photographs or artistic visuals and a less plain, text-heavy Our Story page in this conversation on 4 October 2026. Existing approved text and chapter bodies are retained.
Decision: PUBLISH for this visual presentation change.

## Findings against the 13 audit dimensions

1. Factual accuracy: existing introductory paragraphs retained. New display labels use BF-001, BF-002, BF-004, BF-005 and BF-006 in the Fact Register.
2. Invented facts/history: no origin anecdotes, founder quotes or outcomes added.
3. Source alignment: Fact Register and Brand Foundation govern the short labels; story titles, excerpts and links continue to come from existing Book modules.
4. First-hand evidence: none claimed. Landscape images are visibly described as brand illustrations, not store or supplier photography.
5. Regulated claims: no health, nutrition, investment, price or promotion claims added. Product posters with embedded pricing were excluded.
6. Duplication: existing story routes retained; no duplicated article bodies.
7. Search phrasing: metadata and schema unchanged; no keyword additions.
8. Readability: grouped image cards, bounded text widths, responsive columns, visible section anchor.
9. Grammar and British English: existing copy retained; new English labels use British English, including “colourful”.
10. Cultural accuracy: landscapes present brand atmosphere; no geographic or historical provenance asserted.
11. Usefulness: visitors can open the same complete story collection and the existing Pegasus experience.
12. Brand voice: restrained gold, forest green and warm neutral palette; the official logo is reused without alteration.
13. Readiness: typecheck and targeted lint passed. Both locale pages were rendered locally; all image paths resolve. Production build, public route and browser appearance checks follow deployment.

No new expert or legal review is required for these non-regulated visual changes. This audit does not reapprove or modify the existing chapter manuscripts.
