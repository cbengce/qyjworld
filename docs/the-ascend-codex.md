# The ASCEND Codex™

## Permanent Canon v1.0

**Authority:** This document is the single source of truth for the QING YUN JIAN ASCEND Universe. It governs every future designer, developer, writer, AI system, creative director and production partner.

If a brief, image, article, interface or campaign conflicts with this Codex, the Codex prevails. Changes require a versioned canon amendment approved by the owner. Campaign convenience is never sufficient reason to alter canon.

The ASCEND Universe is a fictional-but-believable cultural landscape. It is not a claim about QING YUN JIAN's legal or corporate history.

## The governing principle

People are not collecting pictures. They are collecting pieces of a world that truly exists.

The world is revealed slowly. Every release adds evidence, atmosphere or relationship; it does not summarize the whole. Mystery comes from restraint and incomplete access, never from magic or incoherence.

# Part I: Canon

## Canon geography

The ASCEND Universe occupies one continuous highland massif. The principal route rises from its southern foothill to its northern summit:

**Origin Mountain → First Spring → The First Path → Tea Ridge → Mist Garden → Morning Lake → Reflection Pool → Hidden Bridge → Silent Valley → Moon Pavilion → Ascending Stair → Cloud Gate → Tea Observatory → Founder Peak**

**East Terrace** is the sole formal detour. It branches from Tea Ridge and rejoins the route at Mist Garden.

Cloud Gate is a natural mountain pass, never a literal or supernatural gate. The water, geology, ecology and elevation relationships defined here cannot change between releases.

## Canonical location records

### 01. Origin Mountain

- **Meaning:** Every ascent begins by understanding why it matters.
- **Purpose:** Establish the world's scale, beginning and moral direction.
- **History:** The southern foothill was the earliest known point of entry into the massif; workers sheltered there before any formal route existed.
- **Present state:** A quiet lower slope of tea growth, old retaining stone and recovering forest; it remains the accepted beginning of the journey.
- **Nearby relationships:** First Spring lies above its eastern fold; Founder Peak is its distant northern crown, visible only in clear weather.
- **Can happen here:** Arrival, orientation, first decisions, small gatherings before departure and stories about purpose.
- **Can never happen here:** A summit ceremony, commercial market, spectacle, noisy festival, monumental gate or claim that the journey is already complete.

### 02. First Spring

- **Meaning:** Intention becomes movement.
- **Purpose:** Give every journey a precise and humble point of beginning.
- **History:** The spring was found before the terraces were established and became the reason the lower route remained in use.
- **Present state:** Clear groundwater emerges beneath a low natural stone ledge and runs south through the foothill forest.
- **Nearby relationships:** Reached from Origin Mountain; The First Path begins beside its outflow.
- **Can happen here:** Filling a cup, washing hands, quiet vows, first meetings and the serving of plain water before tea.
- **Can never happen here:** Bathing, crowds, amplified sound, commerce, decorative fountains, colored lighting or any claim that it is fed by Morning Lake.

### 03. The First Path

- **Meaning:** A decision acquires value through action.
- **Purpose:** Turn a private intention into sustained forward movement.
- **History:** Repeated foot traffic formed the route before irregular local stones were laid to protect it from monsoon erosion.
- **Present state:** A rain-dark path rising through sheltered forest, maintained without being made new.
- **Nearby relationships:** Begins at First Spring and reaches the lower edge of Tea Ridge.
- **Can happen here:** Walking, uncertainty, recommitment, solitary reflection and the first view back toward the foothill.
- **Can never happen here:** Vehicles, races, processions, ornamental paving, shortcuts to the summit or a direct connection to Cloud Gate.

### 04. Tea Ridge

- **Meaning:** Patience becomes visible through repeated work.
- **Purpose:** Hold the world's cultivated tea landscape and its culture of care.
- **History:** The first terraces were established here after the lower path proved reliable through several wet seasons.
- **Present state:** Working tea rows follow the contour of the middle slope, supported by dry-stone walls and narrow footpaths.
- **Nearby relationships:** Receives The First Path; branches east to East Terrace and north-west toward Mist Garden.
- **Can happen here:** Cultivation, harvest, teaching, weather observation, careful work and stories about patience.
- **Can never happen here:** Industrial machinery dominating the landscape, mass tourism, floating ingredients, fantasy agriculture or instant harvests disconnected from season and labor.

### 05. East Terrace

- **Meaning:** Perspective changes when one pauses at the right height.
- **Purpose:** Offer the clearest first-light outlook in the universe.
- **History:** A working ledge used by tea stewards was later stabilized as a restrained viewing terrace.
- **Present state:** A dark-timber deck on a local-stone base faces the eastern cloud field without interrupting the ridge line.
- **Nearby relationships:** Reached by a short branch from Tea Ridge and reconnects with Mist Garden.
- **Can happen here:** Sunrise observation, quiet conversation, planning and the first clear view of the central watershed.
- **Can never happen here:** Sunset identity, markets, banquets, permanent advertising, roofed temple forms or a route directly to Founder Peak.

### 06. Mist Garden

- **Meaning:** Understanding grows through attention to subtle things.
- **Purpose:** Bring tea, weather, scent and cultivation into one sheltered sensory place.
- **History:** Naturally persistent mist protected experimental tea plantings; low beds were shaped gradually around the existing ecology.
- **Present state:** A cool saddle of tea, moss, restrained planting beds and one plain timber bench.
- **Nearby relationships:** Receives both routes from Tea Ridge and East Terrace; opens north toward Morning Lake.
- **Can happen here:** Sensory study, quiet teaching, ingredient research, morning tea and close observation.
- **Can never happen here:** A fantasy garden, luminous plants, ornamental maze, loud event, night market or campaign-driven renaming.

### 07. Morning Lake

- **Meaning:** Clarity returns when movement becomes still.
- **Purpose:** Restore balance at the center of the journey.
- **History:** The rain-fed upland basin predates human use; its shore later became the safest central resting place.
- **Present state:** A calm natural lake reflecting the eastern sky, with one modest stone landing and no permanent building.
- **Nearby relationships:** Reached from Mist Garden; drains through Reflection Pool.
- **Can happen here:** Rest, renewal, silent tea, weather watching and stories about balance.
- **Can never happen here:** Motor craft, entertainment, artificial fountains, built islands, a pavilion, commerce or a claim that First Spring flows from the lake.

### 08. Reflection Pool

- **Meaning:** Honest progress requires an honest view of oneself.
- **Purpose:** Create a deliberate threshold between restoration and deeper inward travel.
- **History:** The lake's outflow was carefully stabilized with fitted local stone to protect the downstream path.
- **Present state:** A shallow, wind-sheltered pool with slow moving water and an understated stone edge.
- **Nearby relationships:** Receives water from Morning Lake and releases it beneath Hidden Bridge.
- **Can happen here:** Private reflection, review, apology, reconsideration and restrained scenes involving Stillearth.
- **Can never happen here:** Divination, magic mirrors, ceremonial circles, crowds, performance, artificial color or the permanent identity of Evenfall.

### 09. Hidden Bridge

- **Meaning:** No meaningful ascent is entirely solitary.
- **Purpose:** Represent trust, connection and passage made possible by others.
- **History:** An earlier seasonal crossing was replaced by one low stone bridge after the outflow repeatedly became impassable.
- **Present state:** The bridge remains partially concealed by lower foliage and carries only foot traffic.
- **Nearby relationships:** Crosses the water leaving Reflection Pool and enters Silent Valley.
- **Can happen here:** Meeting, waiting, helping another traveller, referral stories and moments of shared decision.
- **Can never happen here:** Conflict, toll collection, vehicles, grand entrances, locks of romance, market stalls or relocation to another watercourse.

### 10. Silent Valley

- **Meaning:** Humility begins when noise is no longer available as protection.
- **Purpose:** Remove distraction before the upper ascent.
- **History:** The western hollow was never cultivated heavily because its sheltered acoustics and watercourse were valued as a place of rest.
- **Present state:** Grass, low tea growth and slow ground mist occupy a protected valley with minimal human intervention.
- **Nearby relationships:** Entered from Hidden Bridge; the western trail rises toward Moon Pavilion.
- **Can happen here:** Listening, patience, private grief held without spectacle, rest and Evenfall stories about return.
- **Can never happen here:** Battle, horror, despair as the defining emotion, amplified sound, crowds, festivals, monuments or campaign-driven renaming.

### 11. Moon Pavilion

- **Meaning:** Reflection becomes richer when it can be shared quietly.
- **Purpose:** Give evening tea, belonging and thoughtful conversation a permanent home.
- **History:** A simple rest shelter was built on the western shoulder, fell into disuse and was later restored using its original stone base and dark-timber proportions.
- **Present state:** One open-sided, low-roof tea shelter faces the moonlit valley; its practical warm light is used sparingly.
- **Nearby relationships:** Reached from Silent Valley; the path behind it begins the approach to Ascending Stair.
- **Can happen here:** Evening tea, intimate conversation, reflection before the demanding ascent, Luna Tide stories and small acts of welcome.
- **Can never happen here:** Battle, crowds, markets, noise, modern screens, decorative temple ornament, mystical ritual, nightlife or a second pavilion with a different design.

### 12. Ascending Stair

- **Meaning:** Discipline is the quiet repetition of the next necessary step.
- **Purpose:** Make effort physically unavoidable before reaching exposed high ground.
- **History:** The upper route was stabilized gradually where rain and slope made the original track unsafe.
- **Present state:** Uneven local-stone steps follow the escarpment; wind and elevation increase continuously.
- **Nearby relationships:** Begins above Moon Pavilion and ends at Cloud Gate.
- **Can happen here:** Effort, pause, resilience, failure followed by continuation and stories about consistency.
- **Can never happen here:** Mechanical lifts, effortless shortcuts, competitions, triumphal arches, theatrical lighting or a level route.

### 13. Cloud Gate

- **Meaning:** Hope is the courage to cross beyond familiar shelter.
- **Purpose:** Mark the real geographical transition from protected slope to exposed upper mountain.
- **History:** The natural pass was named by travellers because cloud repeatedly enters through the gap between its two stone shoulders.
- **Present state:** A narrow, wind-exposed passage with low vegetation and no constructed gate.
- **Nearby relationships:** Receives Ascending Stair and opens toward the high ledge of Tea Observatory.
- **Can happen here:** Crossing cloud, change in weather, hesitation, courage, Cloudlift stories and the first full view above inversion.
- **Can never happen here:** Portals, doors, glowing thresholds, magic, carved symbols, guardians, ceremonies or a literal gate structure.

### 14. Tea Observatory

- **Meaning:** Vision begins with careful observation rather than prediction.
- **Purpose:** Unite practical tea knowledge, weather study and responsibility for the future.
- **History:** A small highland research ledge was formalized during the restoration era using contemporary stone-and-timber construction.
- **Present state:** A low-profile belvedere records cloud, wind and tea conditions without dominating the skyline.
- **Nearby relationships:** Reached after Cloud Gate; the final trail continues to Founder Peak.
- **Can happen here:** Study, education, planning, weather records, intergenerational teaching and long-range thinking.
- **Can never happen here:** Astrology, prophecy, surveillance, spectacle, domes, fantasy instruments or claims of controlling weather.

### 15. Founder Peak

- **Meaning:** Achievement creates responsibility for those who come next.
- **Purpose:** Complete the physical ascent while refusing to present the summit as an ending.
- **History:** The highest northern point remained largely untouched; only a low local-stone survey marker records the established route.
- **Present state:** Clean wind, sparse vegetation and wide views connect the summit visually back to Origin Mountain.
- **Nearby relationships:** Reached only from Tea Observatory; overlooks the full route in clear conditions.
- **Can happen here:** Quiet arrival, stewardship commitments, legacy stories, restrained Founder Circle moments and future Golden Tide canon after product approval.
- **Can never happen here:** Conquest, flags, palaces, monuments to ego, parties, markets, permanent accommodation or a claim that ascent ends here.

# Part II: The Canon Timeline

Exact calendar years are deliberately undisclosed. The chronology is canonical; fabricated historical dates are forbidden.

## Era I: Before the Path

1. The massif, lake basin and groundwater system exist without formal human route.
2. First Spring is noticed by travellers sheltering on the southern foothill.
3. Repeated passage creates the earliest trace of The First Path.

## Era II: Cultivation

4. The First Path is stabilized with irregular local stone.
5. Tea terraces are established on Tea Ridge after several wet seasons prove the route reliable.
6. The sheltered plantings that become Mist Garden are tended without replacing the native ecology.
7. Workers begin using East Terrace to read morning weather before harvest.

## Era III: Connection

8. Morning Lake becomes the central resting place for travellers and tea stewards.
9. Reflection Pool is stabilized to protect the outflow and downstream path.
10. Hidden Bridge replaces an unreliable seasonal crossing.
11. A simple shelter is built at Moon Pavilion for evening rest and tea.
12. Upper stones are laid gradually along Ascending Stair.

## Era IV: Quiet Years

13. Use of the upper route declines.
14. Tea cultivation continues, but Moon Pavilion and parts of Ascending Stair fall into disrepair.
15. The natural pass later called Cloud Gate remains known to only a small number of stewards.

## Era V: Restoration

16. The Founder arrives through the southern foothill, follows the surviving route and chooses restoration over replacement.
17. The First Path and Ascending Stair are repaired with local stone.
18. Moon Pavilion is restored to its original footprint and proportions.
19. The first restored cup is served at Moon Pavilion; this is a cultural event within the ASCEND Universe, not a claim about the company's first commercial sale.
20. Tea Observatory is built as a restrained place of study and future stewardship.
21. The full route to Founder Peak is reopened.

## Era VI: The Singapore Chapter

22. The values discovered along the route are interpreted through modern Singapore life and QING YUN JIAN's culture of tea.
23. The Book begins recording selected fragments without publishing the complete map.
24. The first ASCEND profiles connect personal values to permanent locations.
25. The community expands through shared tea, membership, study and gradual discovery.
26. Collectible 001 begins the public sequence; the route remains only partially known.

The Singapore Chapter is the present canon era.

# Part V: Story Progression

## Reveal doctrine

- Public releases reveal fragments, not encyclopedia entries.
- A release should answer one question and quietly create another.
- Geography is learned through repeated evidence: direction, weather, water, material and neighboring landmarks.
- The full map is an internal canon document until an approved story milestone reveals it.
- Mystery never permits contradiction. Unknown is acceptable; impossible is not.

## Long-form reveal architecture

| Milestone | Public knowledge gained |
| --- | --- |
| Collectible 001 | First Spring exists, and every ascent begins with water rather than a summit. |
| Collectible 006 | A stone path rises from the spring, but its destination is not named. |
| Collectible 018 | Origin Mountain and The First Path are understood as parts of one southern beginning. |
| Collectible 021 | Tea Ridge reveals that the journey is sustained by work, not inspiration alone. |
| Collectible 040 | East Terrace and Mist Garden reveal the first branching route. |
| Collectible 063 | A Tea World clue connects Moon Pavilion to Silent Valley without revealing the upper stair. |
| Collectible 090 | The permanent relationships between the Tea Worlds become legible. |
| Collectible 101 | The values are shown to be locations one must revisit, not ranks one completes. |
| Collectible 120 | The route is reinterpreted: ascent is responsibility, and the summit has never been the final purpose. |
| Collectible 135 | The Singapore Chapter reveals that the world continues through community and modern stewardship. |
| Collectible 150 | Founder Peak is connected visually back to Origin Mountain; the first cycle closes while future numbering remains open. |

## Information levels

1. **Atmosphere:** a sound, material, weather state or line of text.
2. **Place:** a location name and one defining value.
3. **Relationship:** evidence that two known locations touch.
4. **Route:** a partial sequence of three or more locations.
5. **Reinterpretation:** a later fact that deepens earlier releases without invalidating them.

No single consumer release should disclose all five levels.

# Part VI: Editorial Rules

## Voice

- Calm, exact and economical.
- Concrete before abstract.
- One memorable thought per release.
- Sensory detail must be physically plausible.
- Confidence without grandiosity.
- Mystery through omission, not vague mystical language.

## Length discipline

- Collectible front: one location, one identity and no more than two short lines of prose.
- Collectible reverse or caption: generally 25-60 words.
- Article opening: locate the reader through one concrete detail before explaining an idea.
- Book chapter: may deepen history but should never exhaust a location's canon.
- Product copy: references a place's emotion or weather without narrating the full lore.

## Approved tonal references

The restraint and editorial confidence may be comparable to Apple, Kinfolk, Aman, Monocle and National Geographic. Their copy, layouts and proprietary language must not be imitated.

## Prohibited editorial behavior

- No fantasy exposition, chosen-one narratives, prophecy or supernatural causation.
- No fake proverbs, fake classical quotations or invented Chinese mythology.
- No breathless luxury claims or excessive adjectives.
- No explaining the entire map in marketing copy.
- No using lore to make medical, historical or corporate claims.
- No treating the reader as a player, warrior, collector rank or game character.

# Part VII: Official Collectible System

## Continuous numbering

| Numbers | Collection | Canon role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 001-020 | Origins | The mountain, spring, first path and reasons for beginning |
| 021-060 | Journey | Work, weather, connection and the route toward higher ground |
| 061-090 | Tea Worlds | Permanent relationships between tea identities and places |
| 091-120 | Ascend Values | The fifteen values and their reinterpretation through lived experience |
| 121-150 | Legacy | Singapore, community, stewardship and the next generation |

## Numbering laws

1. Numbers are issued once and never reused.
2. A revised print keeps its original number and adds a revision mark; it does not receive a new collectible number.
3. Digital, print and event editions of the same canonical release share the same number.
4. A number cannot be published until its location, fact level and continuity dependencies are recorded.
5. Gaps may remain intentionally sealed.
6. Future collections continue at 151. Numbering never restarts for a season, market, format or product.
7. Scarcity may apply to a physical edition, never to access to essential safety or membership information.

## Canon record for every collectible

Each release must internally record:

- number;
- collection;
- canonical location;
- time and weather;
- fact revealed;
- information level;
- previous dependencies;
- future clues enabled;
- official copy;
- approved visual reference;
- revision status.

# Part VIII: Future Expansion

## The Book

Chapters inherit permanent locations and reveal history in route order or purposeful counterpoint. A chapter may revisit a known place from a new time, but cannot alter its canon.

## Website and articles

Metadata, internal links and editorial imagery can use location identities. The public site reveals only approved fragments; internal canon data remains private.

## Membership and rewards

Milestones may represent places reached or values practiced. They must not imply social superiority or turn Founder Peak into a status trophy.

## Physical collectibles, prints and packaging

Every object records a canonical number and location. New compositions show different physically possible viewpoints, not redesigned landmarks.

## Exhibitions and events

Sound, material, lighting and tea service may evoke one location at a time. Events cannot combine incompatible weather, architecture or chronology merely for spectacle.

## Interactive map, AR and mobile application

Digital experiences use the canonical relationship graph, elevation, route and reveal dependencies. Unreleased locations remain obscured rather than procedurally invented.

## Future flagship stores

A store may interpret materials and values from the universe, especially Moon Pavilion, Tea Ridge or Tea Observatory. It must not claim to be the literal fictional location or reproduce fake historical architecture.

Expansion adds viewpoints, stories and participation. It never requires rewriting an established place.

# Part IX: Non-negotiable Rules

Never:

- generate or place fantasy creatures;
- generate, redraw or reinterpret the official Pegasus logo;
- create random temples or decorative pseudo-historic architecture;
- invent fake Oriental mythology, calligraphy or symbols;
- create portals, magic circles, glowing runes or mystical particles;
- imitate tarot, oracle, astrology or trading-card-game aesthetics;
- use anime, game UI, fantasy concept-art or chosen-one language;
- contradict the map, water flow, elevation ecology, timeline or permanent tea assignments;
- change a canonical structure between releases;
- reveal the complete world merely to increase content volume;
- confuse fictional canon with legal company history or factual Singapore history.

Always:

- remain calm, believable, timeless and premium;
- use real environmental cause and effect;
- protect negative space and editorial restraint;
- deepen emotional attachment before increasing quantity;
- reveal another part of the same place rather than another unrelated image;
- record canon dependencies before production begins.

# Canon Governance

## Authority order

1. The latest owner-approved version of **The ASCEND Codex™**.
2. Approved canon amendments.
3. Approved location reference imagery.
4. Production briefs derived from the above.

Earlier geography and visual-development documents are historical working records. Where wording differs, this Codex governs.

## Amendment format

Every amendment must state:

- Codex version;
- date approved;
- exact section changed;
- reason;
- affected locations, releases and systems;
- continuity review result;
- owner approval.

Silent edits are forbidden. Retcons should be avoided; when unavoidable, they require an explicit owner decision and impact review.

## Final production test

Before anything enters production, ask:

1. Does it belong to one canonical location?
2. Could it physically happen there?
3. Does it respect history, present state, neighboring relationships and prohibitions?
4. Does it reveal only what this release is allowed to reveal?
5. Does it deepen the world rather than decorate it?
6. Would a long-term follower recognize the place without a label?
7. Does it remain believable without fantasy explanation?

If any answer is no, the work does not belong to the ASCEND Universe.
