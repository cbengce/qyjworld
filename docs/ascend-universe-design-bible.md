# QING YUN JIAN ASCEND Universe

> **Canon status:** This document records the visual-development system. The authoritative single source of truth is [The ASCEND Codex™](./the-ascend-codex.md). If wording differs, the Codex prevails.

## Permanent visual identity

The ASCEND Universe is a single photographic world. Every release depicts a destination that feels real, quiet, elevated and worth visiting. The image carries the emotion before typography or product information appears.

### Recognition anchors

Every approved visual must share all of these anchors:

- a tactile foreground made from natural stone, water, tea growth or rain-dark timber;
- one unambiguous visual route that guides the eye upward;
- a layered midground shaped by tea terraces, tropical foliage, mountains or mist;
- a calm area of negative space reserved for editorial typography;
- deep forest green and soft jade balanced by stone grey and mist white;
- warm gold used only as believable sunlight or reflection;
- realistic medium-format depth, natural texture and restrained filmic grading;
- silence, purpose, hope and calm confidence.

The repeated world grammar makes the collection recognizable. The destination, season, weather and focal route make each visual distinct.

## Composition system

1. **Foreground:** occupies roughly the lower 35-45 percent. It must be tactile and provide the first step into the scene.
2. **Ascending route:** a path, stair, watercourse, ridge, clearing or light transition must move the eye from lower foreground toward higher ground.
3. **Midground:** provides scale and atmospheric depth without becoming busy.
4. **Background:** resolves into mountains, mist, water, sky or a restrained horizon.
5. **Negative space:** at least one quiet region must remain readable behind editorial copy.
6. **Focal discipline:** one primary visual idea only. Supporting details must not compete with it.
7. **Asymmetry:** compositions should feel naturally balanced, never mirrored or mechanically centered.

## Camera and material rules

- Photographic language: large-format or medium-format travel editorial.
- Default perspective: natural 45-65 mm equivalent; no extreme wide-angle distortion.
- Depth: clear foreground, midground and background with plausible atmospheric perspective.
- Materials: real stone, wet leaves, water, tea plants, bamboo, timber and mist.
- Texture: detailed enough for large print, never over-sharpened or artificially glossy.
- Reflections: physically plausible and restrained.
- No accidental symmetry, synthetic bokeh, fake lens flares or artificial HDR.

## Lighting system

Approved light comes from the natural world:

- morning mist;
- moonlight reflected by water;
- sunrise filtered through cloud;
- rain immediately after dawn;
- cloud inversion;
- diffused highland light;
- soft tropical haze.

Light must reveal texture and direction. It must never appear magical, neon, theatrical or particle-driven. Even moonlit scenes retain visible natural detail and calm hope.

## Color system

| Role | Color family | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Deep forest green | Shadowed foliage and visual gravity |
| Living tone | Soft jade | Tea growth, water and reflected light |
| Structure | Stone grey | Paths, ridges and natural architecture |
| Air | Mist white | Atmosphere, distance and negative space |
| Accent | Warm morning gold | Sunlight and restrained highlights only |
| Secondary | Moon silver | Moonlit reflections and cool air |
| Secondary | Tea brown | Timber and earth |
| Secondary | Muted olive | Transitional foliage |

Avoid neon, fantasy purple, cyber blue, oversaturated orange, artificial teal-orange grading and metallic gold decoration.

## Environmental vocabulary

Approved recurring motifs include tea mountains, stone paths, steps, rain, bamboo shadows, still water, cloud valleys, highland wind, natural reflections and distant horizons. These motifs create continuity without duplicating scenes.

Buildings may appear only when the place requires them and the structure is culturally and geographically grounded. Random temples, ornamental pavilions and invented heritage architecture are not permitted.

## Brand asset rules

- The official Pegasus may appear only as the existing approved logo asset.
- Never generate, trace, redraw, reinterpret or place a Pegasus inside the environment.
- Never generate horses, wings, dragons, phoenixes, fantasy creatures, fake calligraphy or mystical symbols.
- The environment expresses ascent through elevation, distance, paths, light, water, wind and cloud.

## Collections

- **Origins:** beginnings, tea mountains, ancient silence and purpose.
- **Journey:** discipline, patience, rain, wind, steps and higher ground.
- **Tea Worlds:** a distinct emotional destination for each signature drink, without product photography or floating ingredients.
- **Ascend:** integrity, humility, consistency, resilience, focus and courage.
- **Legacy:** Singapore, community, members, students, culture and the next generation.

## Production review gate

Reject an image if any answer is no:

1. Does it look like award-winning photography rather than generated concept art?
2. Does it depict a place someone would wish to visit?
3. Is the composition readable at phone size and printable at exhibition scale?
4. Is there one clear focal route and meaningful negative space?
5. Does it share the ASCEND palette, texture, depth and emotional restraint?
6. Is it free of fantasy, decorative-card and gaming language?
7. Would it stand beside every existing master without breaking the universe?
8. Would someone frame or collect it?

## Master reference set v1.0

The canonical first ten masters are declared in `lib/ascend/universe.ts` and stored under `public/assets/ascend/universe/masters`. They are reference standards for future releases, not an instruction to publish every image immediately.

Future assets must begin from this bible and the relevant master reference. New work must extend the world, not merely imitate a color grade.
